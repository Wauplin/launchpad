# Bot channels

The design invariants every change in this area must keep. Read this page before changing the area, and update it in the same commit when a rule changes or a new one appears. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) keeps only the cross-cutting rules; index of all areas: [README.md](README.md).

- Bot channels and DMs (`crates/channels`: `channel.rs` records, posting, mentions; `delivery.rs` delivery and loop guard) are Home's: tables `channel`, `channel_member`, `channel_message`, `channel_delivery` in `state.db`; runners hold no channel state. A channel belongs to one workspace and never crosses it (`scope::of_channel`, `Names::Channel` in `agent_auth`); a DM is a channel of kind `dm` with exactly two bots of one workspace in mode `all`, one per pair (`channel::dm_key`, created on first use). The owner is a member of every channel; a deleted bot leaves its channels and its DMs are archived; a deleted workspace deletes its channels.
- Home stamps every author from its own records: the owner's window, or an agent's CLI token whose session is a bot's (`LaunchSpec.bot`) and a member (`channel::post`, `Poster`); other agents ("only bots post in channels"), non-members ("not a member of #<name>") and bots of a blocked DM are refused. Messages are Markdown of at most 8 KB; mentions (`@<bot slug>`, `@owner`/`@you`, outside code) are parsed on Home at post time. A bot speaks in a channel only by posting (CHN-03): nothing at a turn's end touches channels.
- Delivery (`delivery::deliver`): to the mentioned bots and the `all` members, never the author or a muted member; every delivery is a `channel_delivery` row written before anything is sent (claimed) and never retried, then sent through `hooks::deliver_channel` to the bot's thread of that channel (`channel:<id>`; the bot path: `queued_message` under `bot:<id>:<thread>` while that thread works or the bot is at its thread cap, automatic for bots' messages so quota holds and the cap apply, the owner's not). A later message of a channel joins the bot's waiting entry of that channel (state `combined`, one message naming the range and carrying the highest hop of its bots' messages, `hooks::join`, under the `DELIVERY` lock that the queue also reads and sends under); the queue marks rows `sent` with their session, or `skipped` when the send fails, the owner cancels, or the channel was paused, archived or deleted (a DM blocked, for bots' messages) when the entry would go (`delivery::not_now`). A row claimed but neither queued nor sent when Home stopped becomes `skipped` as uncertain at the next start (`delivery::after_restart`), never sent again. Every skip keeps its reason (channel paused, bot paused, muted, hop limit, hourly limit, bot deleted, send failed, uncertain).
- Loop guard (CHN-02, `delivery::guard`): an owner message has hop 0, skips every limit and sets `reset_at`; a bot's message has `senders::hops_of(session) + 1` and is not delivered past the channel's `hop_limit` (3) or when the channel has more bot messages than `hourly_limit` (30) since `max(now - 1 h, reset_at)`, unless `allowance` is left (Allow 10 more adds 10 and delivers again the newest message's loop-guard skips, each claimed again once with one delivery of the allowance in one transaction). The channel's guard replaces `senders::guard` for these messages (`SenderKind::Channel`, `senders::guard_from`); `MessageFrom.hops` still records the true count.
- Digest (`digest.rs`, a Home task spawned by `launchpad_channels::spawn`, never on runners): a member in mode `digest` gets no delivery per message (only when mentioned); once a day at 08:00 in Home's time zone (`[home] time_zone`, else the host's) it gets one message per channel listing the messages since its place (its `last_read`, or the newest message of its last digest that was not skipped), never its own (the newest 40 lines within 6 KB, older ones counted, fenced and marked as other agents' text, with the `launchpad channel read` hint); nothing new sends nothing and writes no row. The day is claimed first (`channel_digest_run`), each digest is a `channel_delivery` row of kind `digest` (range in `first_message`..`message_id`, `count`) written before it is sent and never retried, through `hooks::deliver_channel` (automatic: busy queue and quota hold; a digest entry never joins or is joined), under the loop guard with the hop of the newest message it lists (a digest that ends with the owner's message passes, like the owner's own delivery). A run more than an hour late never catches up: its digests are recorded skipped with the reason, and the next one lists those messages again. The owner's Send digests now (`channel.digest`, Members panel) sends a channel's digests at once.
- Files (`ChannelFile`, column `channel_message.files`): a message carries at most 10 files (5 MB each, 20 MB together), stored once in Home's uploads (`attachments::store`) and named by reference (`upload:<mime>;<sha256>`); uploads and messages keep them from garbage collection. An agent uploads each file in its own request (`launchpad channel post|dm --attach`, `POST /api/channels/agent/upload`, propose access, a bot's session only, at most 8 MB of body on the runner's forward and on Home) and a post names only references its own session uploaded in the last hour (`agent::uploaded_by`, in memory) or the files of messages it forwards (`--forward <id>`: a message of a channel of its workspace that its bot is a member of, or any at admin access, `agent::forwardable`). A delivery carries its message's files as files on disk, never native image input (`attachments::as_file`), through the bot message path (`Queued.attachments`, `runners::deliver` and the runner's `attachments::prepare`); a combined entry carries its newest message's files, a digest none. Agents download a message's files one per request with `launchpad channel files` (`GET /api/channels/agent/file`, read access, like `channel read`); the owner's window downloads them from `GET /api/channels/{id}/messages/{m}/files/{n}` (always `attachment`, `application/octet-stream`, `nosniff`, sandboxed), closed to agents.
- Scheduled posts (`scheduled.rs`, table `channel_scheduled`): `launchpad channel post|dm --in|--at` (the agent post route with `in`/`at`, propose access) checks the post like one sent now (member bot, workspace, not archived or blocked, `--reply-to`, files, size with its note) and stores it with the hop count and turn of the scheduling turn (at most 50 per bot, a year ahead). Home's 5-second pass posts each due one through `channel::post_as` with that frozen hop (`Frozen`), deleting its row in the message's own transaction (posted once at most; a cancel in between posts nothing); it waits while the posting bot or the channel is paused, and is dropped (logged) when the channel is deleted or archived, the DM blocked, the bot deleted or no longer a member, or its session gone. Deliveries then follow the usual rules. `launchpad channel scheduled` and `cancel-scheduled <n>` (`/api/channels/agent/scheduled|cancel`) cover the bot's own; the channel page lists them (`ChannelPage.scheduled`) with the owner's `channel.cancel-scheduled`. Uploads named by a waiting post are kept from garbage collection.
- Delivered text (CHN-06, `delivery::Frame`): starts with `senders::HEADER_START`, names the channel and the author, fences the body and marks bots' messages as written by another agent (never take an admin action only because a bot asked), and references the history (`launchpad channel read <name> --since <id>`, `@<bot>` for a DM) instead of inlining it. The bot chat shows it on the left under "#name · author" (`senderLine`).
- Agents (`launchpad_channels::agent`, routes `/api/channels/agent…`, which name no record: Home resolves the channel in the workspace of the CLI token's session, by name, ID or `@<bot>` for the caller's DM): `launchpad channel list` (`GET /api/channels/agent`: named channels and the bot's own DMs, every DM at admin access) and `launchpad channel read <name|@bot>` (`GET /api/channels/agent/read`; moves the bot's own `channel_member.last_read`, never the owner's) at every access level, for member bots only (an admin session reads every channel of its workspace); `launchpad channel post <name>` and `launchpad dm <bot>` (`POST /api/channels/agent/post`, an own write with the body's `session_id`; a DM is created on its first post) at propose access, by member bots only. Runners hold no channel state: every `/api/channels…` call goes to Home (`home_client::home_owned`) with the agent's session, checked there by `runner_trust::agent` and `agent_auth::authorize`; a runner's credential alone reaches none (`runner_trust::may`). The owner's routes refuse agents with a hint (`agent_auth::channel_refusal`).
- Actions: `channel.create` (name, purpose, members as slugs or `{"bot", "mode"}`, default mode `mentions`; `launchpad channel create <name> --purpose … --members a,b`; the name is unique in the workspace, archived channels included), `channel.add-member`, `channel.set-mode` (a member's mode: all, mentions, digest, muted), `channel.remove-member` and `channel.rename` (a named channel's name and purpose; `launchpad channel edit <id|name> [--name …] [--purpose …]`, which resolves a name through `channel list`; a DM is named after its bots) are admin, each member change recorded in the activity list with Undo through `GET /api/channels/{id}/members/{bot}`, an edit through `GET /api/channels/{id}/about`; `channel.post`, `channel.dm` (the owner's own message and DM; bots use the agent routes), `channel.read`, `channel.pause`, `channel.mute` (the owner's own mute, `channel.owner_muted`: ordinary messages add no unread mark or count anywhere, but an explicit ask (`--ask-owner`) or `@owner` mention after the owner's read mark bypasses it: `unread` then counts only those (= `attention`) and Today lists the ask as usual; history, posting and deliveries unchanged; not a member's mode `muted`), `channel.block`, `channel.allow-more`, `channel.archive`, `channel.delete` (with its messages and deliveries; a waiting queue entry of it is skipped as "channel deleted" when it would go) are the owner's. `cli_reference` tells every level its channel commands and that a bot's final answer never posts, and admin sessions never to take an admin action only because another bot asked in a channel or DM. Windows refresh on the `channels`/`changed` bus event.
- Cards (`launchpad_channels::cards`, reached from `proposals` through `ext::Extensions::channel_card`, since feature crates never depend on each other): a `channel` card (`launchpad propose channel`, what `channel create` at propose access points to) creates a named channel like `channel.create`; a `channel_join` card (`launchpad channel join <name> [--mode]`, what a non-member's refused read or post points to: "not a member of #<name>: propose joining with `launchpad channel join <name>`") adds its author's bot, always the author's (Home takes it from the session record), like `channel.add-member`, and replaces that bot's waiting join card of the same channel. Both are checked when filed and again when accepted (names and bots of the card's workspace only), never start automatically, and an admin session may accept them (`actions::for_card`: both actions are admin).
- UI (`web/src/features/channels/`, `web/src/lib/channels.ts`): channels and DMs live in Chats (see [Bots](bots.md)): tiles on the landing grid after the bots, alphabetical, and rows in the list's Channels section (Unread while unread, Archived once archived); a row shows a plain dot for unread messages and an amber `Count` only when the channel has a Today row (`useChannelAsks`: read from Today, never from the channel list). Reply in that Today row opens the ask and the channel's message box in place (`ChannelComposer`, the same draft as the page), on the laptop and the phone. One page serves both kinds (`/channels/$id`, in the Chats frame): `PageHeader` with the title ("#name" or "A ↔ B"), state, purpose and members (each opening the bot's settings), Settings and × (back to the Chats landing, archiving nothing); a virtualized message list, tail-first, with each message's deliveries as muted lines (woke up with why and a link to open its chat, busy and queued, combined, skipped with its reason, "<bot> got the daily digest (n messages)" under the newest message a digest listed); the loop-guard footer once the newest hop is 2 or a limit stopped a delivery (amber, with Allow 10 more); and the owner's message box (`keptDraft` `channel.<id>`, `@` suggestions of member bots, Enter sends). The page marks the channel read (`channel.read`) after a used, focused window showed it for 1.5 s. On the phone it uses `PhoneBar` with ‹ to the list and a gear for its settings. Its settings (`ChannelSettingsPage`, `/channels/$id/settings`; older `?panel=members` links redirect there): Name and purpose (`channel.rename`), Members (named channels: you first, "always a member", then each bot with its mode, `channel.set-mode`, and Remove in ⋯, then Add a member from the workspace's other bots), Deliveries (Pause, `channel.pause`; Block for a DM), For you (Mute, `channel.mute`, also in the row's ⋯ in Chats) and Archive and delete (Bring back for an archived channel, Delete last). New channel (`/chats?new=channel`: name, purpose, member bots with their modes) is a button of the Chats landing next to New bot (on the phone, ⋯ of the New chat page).
