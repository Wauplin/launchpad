Your ordinary data was recovered.
Credentials need attention. Existing encrypted generations are preserved. Start a new credential generation, then reconnect your tools.
Trace export is separate from machine recovery. Its status is in Launchpad → Settings → Hugging Face. Running processes and terminal screens cannot be restored.


Updates & maintenance
Release notes ↗
Prepare update…
A verified clean checkpoint comes first. Agents and terminals must be idle. Updating rebuilds the Space and reconnects this browser.
Image details
Restart Space…
Stop agents and terminals first. A clean checkpoint will be verified before the restart.
Rotate credential encryption key…
Re-encrypt retained credential generations, verify new copies and save a new Space Secret. Agents and terminals must be idle. The Space restarts. All old ciphertext is retained.
Roll back image & data…
Restore the previous image and its pre-update checkpoint. Changes made after that checkpoint will be lost.
