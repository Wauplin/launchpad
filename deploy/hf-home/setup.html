Set up Launchpad Home

Home keeps your sessions, branches and settings in its private bucket, encrypted. Agents run on the runners you pair. Three sign-ins are needed before Home opens.

  1. 1

    Hugging Face

    Home uses this login for its private bucket . Right after it, Home creates an encryption key, keeps it as the LP_HOME_KEY secret of this Space, and restarts once. Everything Home stores in the bucket is then encrypted with it: backups, the vault and its own logins.

    Open the Space settings, add a secret named LP_HOME_KEY with this value, then restart the Space. Keep a copy somewhere safe: without it, the backups cannot be read.
  2. 2

    GitHub

    Used by the Inbox, issues, pull requests, private clones and publication. Home keeps this login encrypted in its bucket. Agents never get it: they get their own tokens from Credentials and tools.

  3. 3

    First profile

    A profile is how agents run. Sign in with your subscription: Home keeps the login encrypted in its vault and lends it to runners for each session.
